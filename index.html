An organization, not a team

BONTEN eSports is a Free Fire organization. We run several teams under one name, with management, funded scrims, our own tournaments and support for every player.

Players first join a team for trials. After the trials, players who make it are placed into the organization’s teams.

What BONTEN eSports gives every player who clears the trials.

Guild entry

Pass the trials and you join our guild.

Funded scrims

We fund your scrims, so you can practice against strong teams.

Friendly environment

A supportive, respectful organization where players grow together.

Trial process

From the form to the trial. Expect 2 to 3 weeks, sometimes longer.

  1. Fill the full form

    Every member of your team must fill it.

  2. We contact you

    Our team reaches out to you after the form.

  3. You get a team

    You are placed in a team and start playing together.

  4. Practice and progress review

    We watch your progress while you practice.

  5. Trial

    When you are ready, you take the trial. Date, time and mode will be shared with applicants.

Disclaimer: we cannot confirm that you will join the organization. It depends on how you perform in the trial.

Common questions

Quick answers before you apply.

How long does the process take?

Usually 2 to 3 weeks from the form to the trial, sometimes longer.

Is joining guaranteed?

No. Joining BONTEN eSports depends on how you perform in the trial.

Is there a salary?

There is currently no fixed salary. Players receive a percentage of earnings.

Is age verification required?

Yes. Age verification is compulsory for every player.

Can I leave after I am placed in a team?

Yes, but you must follow the proper process with management. Do not leave or disappear without informing us.

Terms and conditions

By applying to BONTEN eSports you agree to the following.

  1. Age verification is compulsory for every player.
  2. Fill in all details truthfully. False information leads to rejection.
  3. Once you are assigned to a team, you cannot leave or disappear without informing us.
  4. If you want to leave the organization, you must follow the proper process with management.
  5. Communicate well and respectfully with your teammates and with other teams.
  6. Do not argue or behave rudely with any org’s partners, hosts or founders.
  7. Anyone caught using a hack panel or cheats is banned from the organization directly and permanently. A public ban notice may be issued.
  8. Players receive only a percentage of earnings.
  9. There is currently no fixed salary.
  10. Be online on time for scrims.
  11. Joining the organization is not guaranteed. It depends on your trial performance.
