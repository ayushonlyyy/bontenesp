BONTEN is an organization, not a single team.

We run several Free Fire teams under one name, with management, funded scrims and structured trials. Players join a team for trials first, and those who clear them are placed into BONTEN's teams.

Why BONTEN

What players receive once they clear the trials.

01

Funded scrims

Scrims against strong teams, funded by the organization.

  1. 02

    Guild entry

    Players who clear the trials are admitted to the BONTEN guild.

  2. 03

    Earnings share

    A percentage of weekend earnings is shared among players. There is no fixed salary.

  3. 04

    Sunday friendly rooms

    Friendly rooms with BONTEN teams every Sunday.

  4. 05

    Free scrim slots

    From December, BONTEN teams receive free slots in the organization's own scrims.

  5. 06

    Player eSports card

    A QR card listing tournaments played, wins, biggest prize, experience, device and current organization.

  6. 07

    YouTube features

    Selected gameplay may be featured on BONTEN's YouTube channel in the future.

  7. 08

    Path to larger organizations

    When a squad is ready for a larger organization, management can help facilitate a mutually agreed transition.

  8. 09

    Team culture

    A competitive environment built on teamwork and mutual respect.

  9. 10

    Organizational support

    Players can reach management for support when needed.

Questions

How long does the process take?

Two to three weeks from the form to the trial, sometimes longer.

Is selection guaranteed?

No. It depends on your trial performance.

Do players receive a salary?

There is no fixed salary. Players receive a percentage of earnings.

Is age verification required?

Yes, for every player.

Can I leave after I am placed in a team?

Yes, through the proper process with management. Do not leave without informing us.

Terms and conditions

By applying to BONTEN eSports, you agree to the following.

  1. Age verification is compulsory for every player.
  2. All details must be accurate. False information leads to rejection.
  3. Once you are assigned to a team, you may not leave or disappear without informing management.
  4. To leave the organization, follow the proper process with management.
  5. Communicate respectfully with your teammates and with other teams.
  6. Do not argue with, or behave rudely toward, any partner, host or founder of the organization.
  7. Players caught using a hack panel or cheats are banned from the organization permanently and without appeal. A public ban notice may be issued.
  8. Players receive a percentage of earnings only. There is currently no fixed salary.
  9. Be online on time for scrims.
  10. Joining is not guaranteed. It depends on your trial performance.
