An organization, not a team

BONTEN eSports runs several teams under one name, with management, funded scrims and support for every player.

You join a team for trials first. Players who clear the trials are placed into the organization's teams.

Trial process

Expect 2 to 3 weeks, sometimes longer.

  1. Fill the full form

    Every member of your team must fill it.

  2. We contact you

    Our team reaches out after the form.

  3. You get a team

    You are placed in a team and start playing together.

  4. Practice and review

    We watch your progress while you practice.

  5. Trial

    Date, time and mode are shared with applicants.

Disclaimer: joining is not guaranteed. It depends on your trial performance.

December scrims

Our own scrims start in December. Our teams get free slots. The schedule will be announced.

Questions

How long does the process take?

Usually 2 to 3 weeks from the form to the trial, sometimes longer.

Is joining guaranteed?

No. It depends on how you perform in the trial.

Is there a salary?

No fixed salary right now. Players receive a percentage of earnings.

Is age verification required?

Yes. It is compulsory for every player.

Can I leave after I am placed in a team?

Yes, but follow the proper process with management. Do not leave or disappear without informing us.

Terms and conditions

By applying to BONTEN eSports you agree to the following.

  1. Age verification is compulsory for every player.
  2. Fill in all details truthfully. False information leads to rejection.
  3. Once you are assigned to a team, you cannot leave or disappear without informing us.
  4. If you want to leave the organization, you must follow the proper process with management.
  5. Communicate well and respectfully with your teammates and with other teams.
  6. Do not argue or behave rudely with any org’s partners, hosts or founders.
  7. Anyone caught using a hack panel or cheats is banned from the organization directly and permanently. A public ban notice may be issued.
  8. Players receive only a percentage of earnings.
  9. There is currently no fixed salary.
  10. Be online on time for scrims.
  11. Joining the organization is not guaranteed. It depends on your trial performance.
